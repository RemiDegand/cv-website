Sales rep

Nobody could answer quickly. This project gives both of them the answer on one screen.

Context

In 3 of OSLO's business units, there were hundreds of clients and thousands of invoices a year, but the finance department's accounting tool only showed the total overdue and a raw list of unpaid invoices.

The problem

  • With this many clients and invoices, it was hard to see quickly which disputes to handle first and what action to take.
  • Unpaid invoices built up over time, with no monitoring tool and no clear owner in the sales teams.

Impact & tools

Impact

  • The manager has a high-level view of each sales representative's receivables and sees quickly where the cash is.
  • Each sales representative has the list of their unpaid invoices, sorted by days overdue, with the client and contact details, so they can call the right client straight away.

Receivables are now managed proactively, and the sales teams are accountable for resolving their disputes, contributing to cash control for each business unit.

Tools

  • MyReport Data (Data modeling)
  • MyReport Page (Data visualization)
  • SQL
