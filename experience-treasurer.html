My mission

Context

Envie d'Sup is a public-interest association supported by the French government, with a public budget of €49k. It runs workshops, cultural outings and trips for 229 middle and high school students from priority education zones.

Mission

Pay and track every expense of the association, assess the budget for the next year and ensure a clean handover to the next treasurer.

2

Part 2 of 3

My tasks

  • Built and monitored budget forecasts in a Google Sheets tracker of spending by class, compared with last year's spending, to know how much could still be spent within each allocated budget.
  • Kept a receipt for every expense and closed the accounts in good order at the end of the year.
  • Analysed costs by class and activity type (workshops, cultural outings, trips), to know the resources needed for each student and prepare the next year's grant applications.
