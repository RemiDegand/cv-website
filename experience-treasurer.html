My mission

Ensure the rigorous financial management of the association, for 229 students from priority education zones.

Envie d'Sup is a public-interest association supported by the French government, with a public budget of €49k. It runs workshops, cultural outings and trips for middle and high school students.

  • Pay the supplier invoices.
  • Keep a receipt for every expense and track the spending.
  • Close the accounts in good order, for a clean handover to the next treasurer.
  • Assess the budget needed for the next year.
