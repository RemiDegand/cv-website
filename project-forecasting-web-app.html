Problem & context

How it started

My own idea: see all the zones at the same time, with alarms. I kept developing it, and it became a full web app.

Context

Every week, the forecasting team forecast rider supply for each zone. Forecast accuracy was becoming more and more important: the team had to see the zones that deviated and explain why. We used a very global dashboard on Looker, not dedicated to forecast accuracy.

Accuracy was measured with the Utilization Rate, the average number of orders delivered by one rider in one hour, which gives each time slot one of 3 results:

  • Proper FCUtilization Rate close to its target
  • Under FCToo few slots opened, Utilization Rate above its target
  • Over FCToo many slots opened, Utilization Rate below its target

The problem

  • No quick view of all the zones, no alerts: forecasters selected them one by one, from the most important to the least, on Looker.
  • Hard to find patterns: on Looker, data were aggregated as an average or a sum, depending on the metric, so a recurring pattern by day or by time slot stayed hidden.
  • Too general: root causes (Missing Capacity, Delivery Time, Orders FC deviation) sat on other dashboards, not linked to forecast accuracy.

What I built: one app to understand forecast accuracy

The app at work

Every dashboard, metric and summary answers one question: why did Proper, Under or Over FC move? Other metrics are there for their link to it.

AIHow the summaries are made

  1. A dedicated queryCollects the required KPIs
  2. A file for the AILinks between the KPIs, examples of analysis, rules
  3. One goalExplain the variation of Proper, Under and Over FC

Automated with Python: every Monday morning for the last complete week, and at 9:00 from Monday to Friday for the last 7 days.

Impact & tools

Impact

  • For the whole team: a more organised and quicker way to check the performance of the zones.
  • A new view, FC Schedule: forecast accuracy by day and by time slot, where Looker only gave an average.
  • A new metric, Broken Correlation %: how much was delivered in bad conditions (Delivery Time above target and Utilization Rate below target). A high figure means bad conditions with regard to the target, or a target that cannot be reached.
