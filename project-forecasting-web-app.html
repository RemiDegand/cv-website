Problem & context

How it started

Context

Every week, the forecasting team forecast rider supply for each zone. Forecast accuracy was becoming more and more important: the team had to see the zones that deviated and explain why. We used a very global dashboard on Looker, not dedicated to forecast accuracy.

Accuracy was measured with the Utilization Rate, the average number of orders delivered by one rider in one hour, which gives each time slot one of 3 results:

  • Proper FCUtilization Rate close to its target
  • Under FCToo few slots opened, Utilization Rate above its target
  • Over FCToo many slots opened, Utilization Rate below its target

The problem

  • No quick view of all the zones, no alerts: forecasters selected them one by one, from the most important to the least, on Looker.
  • Hard to find patterns: on Looker, data were aggregated as an average or a sum, depending on the metric, so a recurring pattern by day or by time slot stayed hidden.
  • Not oriented to forecast accuracy: metrics like Delivery Time or Missing Capacity were shown on their own, not to explain why Proper, Under or Over FC moved.

What I built: one app to understand forecast accuracy

1Reporting

The web app and its dashboards

Every dashboard, metric and summary answers one question: why did Proper, Under or Over FC move? Other metrics are there for their link to it.

  • Global Zones: all the zones in one table, filtered by area, country, city or zone.
  • DT Alerts: the zones too far from their Delivery Time target, with thresholds that can be changed.
  • Zone Deep Dive: accuracy by day and by time slot, and a comment box saved automatically.
  • Two periods: last complete week or last 7 days, on every view.
  • Two versions: first the PLECA market, a Google Apps Script web app reading a Google Sheet filled by BigQuery queries; then all markets, hosted on Google Cloud Run with the code in Python.

2AI summaries

Explaining why forecast accuracy moved

A written summary for each zone, with one goal: explain the WoW variation of Proper, Under and Over FC.

  1. A BigQuery queryAll the data needed to explain the variation
  2. An AI agentGiven access to this query
  3. Context and examplesHow to read the data, with examples of analysis
  4. A Python scriptSets when it runs, runs everything, keeps a token limit

It runs every Monday morning for the last complete week, and at 9:00 from Monday to Friday for the last 7 days.

3Chatbot

How the data are computed and how to read them

The metrics were complex, and some were new, so a chatbot explains them.

  1. A question in the appThe "?" button opens Metric Help on every screen
  2. The queriesHow each metric is computed
  3. Complementary informationOnly what the queries don't say, to save tokens
  4. Gemini (Vertex AI)Answers definitions, formulas, aggregations and data sources only

Impact & tools

Impact

  • For the whole team: a more organised and quicker way to check the performance of the zones.
  • A new view, FC Schedule: forecast accuracy by day and by time slot, where Looker only gave an average.
  • A new metric, Broken Correlation %: how much was delivered in bad conditions (Delivery Time above target and Utilization Rate below target). A high figure means bad conditions with regard to the target, or a target that cannot be reached.
