One sheet per quarter, all in one file: it slowed down, then crashed.

Calculating the cash flow had to become quick and reliable.

Context

OSLO, with about €10M revenue a year, calculated its cash flow and that of 2 other entities in an Excel file, fed with accounting entries exported from the ERP as CSV files, pasted in, then completed with complementary manual entries.

The problem

  • Heavy and unstable: every year's extractions were stored in the same file, which crashed regularly.
  • Copy-paste and complementary manual entries every time: a risk of errors (wrong sheet, missing or duplicated rows, typing mistakes), so the cash flow was hard to trust.
  • Time-consuming: the process did not suit regular use.

After

  2. RefreshMyReport pulls the day's entries from the ERP, nothing stored
  3. Cash flow readyWith checks and financial ratios

Impact & tools

Impact

  • A structured process: the entries load straight from the ERP for any period, with no copy-paste, manual entries or stored extractions. The file stays light and no longer crashes, and typing mistakes are gone.
  • Checks at every step: each stage of the calculation has its own control that must be 0, so a mistake shows up at once, along with when and where it happened.
  • Ratios: profitability and the current ratio complement the cash flow, for a fuller view of the company's financial health.
