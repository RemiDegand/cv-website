My tasks

Task 1Forecast rider demand, every week

The forecast had to be ready before the release of the rider slot calendar.

  • Reviewed last week's performance of my 5 countries: what happened (descriptive analysis) and why (diagnostic analysis), crossing Looker dashboards with queries analysed in Google Sheets.
  • Collected input from the 5 local teams (in touch all week, 1 meeting a week with each): the external factors behind past performance and the coming events, such as bad weather, the World Cup or a marketing campaign.
  • Built forecasts for specific events from past performance: marketing campaigns, and bank holidays from year-over-year performance.
