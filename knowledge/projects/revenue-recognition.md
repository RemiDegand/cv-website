# Revenue recognition model (OSLO)

## Summary
At OSLO, Rémi redesigned a revenue recognition data model to allocate revenue month by month based on actual business activity, reducing annual revenue discrepancies from €350k to €8k.

## Context
Rémi was Data Analyst Intern in OSLO's Information Systems department, supporting financial and operational decisions across 4 business units and 3 entities (13 data models, 6 dashboards and 6 automated Excel tools in total). This project is part of the "financial tools & operations" work.

## Problem
The previous revenue recognition model did not allocate revenue according to actual business activity, which led to annual discrepancies of about €350k.

## What I built
A redesigned revenue recognition data model that allocates revenue monthly, based on actual business activity.

## Results and impact
Annual revenue discrepancies reduced from €350k to €8k.
