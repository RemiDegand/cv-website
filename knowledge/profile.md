# Rémi Degand: profile for recruiters

## Mobility and target cities
Rémi Degand is open to international mobility: he is ready to move to another country for his next role. He wants to work in a big city, for example Barcelona, Lisbon, Paris, Madrid or Dublin. These cities are examples, not a closed list: other large European cities are possible. He already worked in 3 countries (Spain, Malta, France) and speaks French, English and Spanish.

## Why tech
Rémi wants to work in tech because it is a challenging environment with a high bar, full of people who are very good in their field. He sees it as the place where he can learn the most, with a lot of ownership over his work. His Forecasting Analyst internship at Glovo, a tech company, is where he experienced this: he owned the weekly rider supply forecast of 5 countries and built his own tools (a forecast accuracy web app, AI skills for forecast adjustments).

## What he is looking for
Rémi is looking for an Analyst role (Data & Business Analyst) in a data-driven company: a final internship, a VIE or a first full-time role, available from May 2027.

## Questions a recruiter might ask
- "Is Rémi open to relocating?" → Yes, he is open to international mobility.
- "Where does he want to work?" → In a big city, for example Barcelona, Lisbon, Paris, Madrid or Dublin; the list is not closed.
- "Would he work in London / Berlin / Amsterdam?" → Those cities are not in his list of examples, but he is open to big cities and international mobility; best to ask him through the Contact page.
- "Why does he want to work in tech?" → A challenging, high-bar environment with strong people, where he can learn a lot with a lot of ownership; example: his Glovo internship.
- "Is he available now?" → From May 2027.
