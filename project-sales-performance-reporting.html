The production plan had to be rebuilt before any reporting could be trusted.

Context

In one of OSLO's business units, selling payment solutions to several hundred clients for about €3M a year, every order was tracked in an Excel production plan shared by the whole team.

The problem

  • Hard to analyse: several tables, one per order status, on the same sheet.
  • No control: everyone could edit the whole file, so changes went unchecked.
  • No reliable statistics to steer the business.

Impact & tools

Impact

  • The manager follows revenue, margin, orders and the target for the whole business unit, by month, quarter and year.
  • Each sales representative sees their own performance, year-to-date statistics and client base.
  • Production managers and sales administration work in one production plan, each on their own columns.

Production data is now reliable, and sales performance is steered with a clear view of the business unit's activity.

Tools

  • Excel
  • MyReport Data (Data modeling)
  • MyReport Page (Data visualization)
